Dependent - Required Documents

Jli
Japan life info
January 31, 2026

The Dependent visa status can be obtained by the spouse or children of foreign nationals residing in Japan with work or student visa status.

Applicable ActivitiesDaily activities as a spouse or child who is supported by a person residing in Japan with a visa status listed in Appended Table I (excluding Diplomatic, Official, Specified Skilled Worker (i), and Technical Intern Training) or Student status
Period of StayA period individually designated by the Minister of Justice (not exceeding 5 years)

Important Notes Before Applying

  • For questions about application forms and required documents, please contact the Immigration Information Center. (TEL: 0570-013904)
  • Applications with incomplete documents may result in significant delays or denial of permission.
  • All certificates issued in Japan must be submitted within 3 months of the date of issue.
  • Documents written in foreign languages must be accompanied by a Japanese translation.
  • Submitted documents will not be returned in principle.

Application for Certificate of Eligibility (New)

This application is for those who wish to enter Japan with Dependent status for the first time.

Documents Proving Supporter’s Occupation and Income

If the supporter engages in income-generating activities:

  • Certificate of employment or copy of business permit (document proving occupation)
  • Residence tax certificate (taxation/non-taxation) and tax payment certificate (showing total income and tax payment status for one year)

If the supporter engages in other activities (e.g., student):

  • Bank balance certificate in the supporter’s name
  • Or scholarship payment certificate stating the amount and period
  • Other documents proving ability to cover the applicant’s living expenses

Important Notes

  • If the supporter’s visa status is revoked, the Dependent status will also be affected.
  • The Dependent visa status generally prohibits employment. To work part-time, you must obtain Permission to Engage in Activities Other Than Those Permitted Under the Status of Residence Previously Granted.
