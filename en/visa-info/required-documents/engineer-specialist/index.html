Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services - Required Documents

January 31, 2026

Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services is the most commonly applied work-related visa status in Japan. It is obtained by those engaged in work requiring specialized knowledge or skills, such as IT engineers, interpreters, translators, marketing, and trade operations.

Applicable ActivitiesActivities involving work requiring knowledge in natural sciences (science, engineering, etc.) or humanities (law, economics, sociology, etc.), or work requiring thinking or sensitivity based on foreign culture, based on contracts with public or private organizations in Japan
Period of Stay5 years, 3 years, 1 year, 3 months

Important Notes Before Applying

  • For questions about application forms and required documents, please contact the Immigration Information Center. (TEL: 0570-013904)
  • Applications with incomplete documents may result in significant delays or denial of permission.
  • Documents written in foreign languages must be accompanied by a Japanese translation.
  • Required documents vary by category. Please confirm your applicable category before submitting documents.

Category Classification of Affiliated Organizations

CategoryApplicable Organizations
Category 1Listed companies, mutual insurance companies, national/local governments, independent administrative agencies, special corporations/authorized corporations, companies eligible for bonus points under the highly skilled professional system, etc.
Category 2Organizations/individuals with total withholding tax of 10 million yen or more for employee salary income in the previous year, or organizations approved to use the online residence application system
Category 3Organizations/individuals that submitted the legal records summary table for employee salary income withholding statements for the previous year (excluding Category 2)
Category 4Organizations/individuals other than Categories 1-3
