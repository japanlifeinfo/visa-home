Immigration Bureau Application Locations by Residence

February 7, 2026

Residence status (visa) applications can only be made at the Immigration Services Agency office that has jurisdiction over your place of residence. If you visit an office that does not have jurisdiction, your application will be rejected, so please make sure to confirm which office covers your residential address before visiting.

If you have any questions, you can call the Foreign Residents Information Center.

  • Phone: 0570-013904 (IP phone: 03-5796-7112)
  • Languages: Japanese, English, Chinese, Korean, etc.
  • Weekdays 8:30-17:15

Immigration Bureau by Place of Residence

The office with jurisdiction is determined based on your place of residence (registered address). You can also apply at nearby branch offices whose jurisdiction overlaps with the main office.

Notes

  • Overlapping jurisdictions: Some branch offices have jurisdiction over multiple prefectures. If multiple branch offices can handle your application, you may choose the nearest one.
  • Office hours: All offices are open weekdays 9:00-12:00, 13:00-16:00 (closed on weekends and public holidays)
  • Information may change: Addresses, phone numbers, and jurisdictions may be subject to change. Please verify the latest information before visiting.
  • Airport/port-only branch offices: Branch offices that only handle airport/port operations (e.g., 仙台空港(Sendai Airport), 福岡空港(Fukuoka Airport), 博多港(Hakata Port), 那覇空港(Naha Airport)) are excluded from this list as they are not directly related to visa/residence status applications.
